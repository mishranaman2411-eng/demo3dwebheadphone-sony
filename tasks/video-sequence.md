## 4. Product Verification (Sony WH-CH520)

### Verified Authenticity:
The footage was verified against official Sony WH-CH520 technical product specifications:
1. **On-Ear Form Factor:** Correct supra-aural swivel earcups with compact dimensions.
2. **Button Cluster:** Exact arrangement of 3 physical tactile buttons on the right cup edge (+ / Play / -) matching Sony's hardware layout.
3. **Connectivity & Ports:** Single USB-C port situated adjacent to the microphone aperture on the right earcup (no 3.5mm analog jack, which is true to the actual WH-CH520 wireless-only design).
4. **Branding:** Precise lowercase-grotesque "SONY" logo engraving above the headband swivel joint.
5. **Driver Specification:** 30mm dynamic driver unit correctly visualized in the internal acoustic teardown.
6. **Feature Alignments:**
   - DSEE sound upscaling
   - 360 Reality Audio certified
   - Multipoint dual Bluetooth connection
   - Lightweight adjustable headband with notched sizing
   - ~50-hour battery life capability (indicated by internal battery cell packaging in Clip 2)

### Discrepancies & Notes:
- **Colorway:** The 3D rendered model in the video is the **Cream / Off-White** variant. The website can present color toggles (Black, White/Cream, Blue, Beige) with this footage serving as the master showcase for the White/Cream model.
- **Tail Frame Hold in Clip 8 (`10.mp4`):** The headphone ascends out of view by frame 120 (~5.0s). The remaining 5.0 seconds is empty black space. The playback controller should pause or transition to the static hero/CTA section once the headphone exits the top boundary.
